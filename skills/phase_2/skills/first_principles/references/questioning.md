Read this before the first run of step 3 in `SKILL.md`. It holds the stems, the cross-check against Paul and Elder, the gap check and the ask-or-answer rule. Source numbers and marks are in `references/sources.md` and in `SKILL.md`.

## Where the list comes from

- The FS page gives six steps as the usual process. Step 1 also asks where your ideas come from. It gives 13 question stems under the six steps [1 C09 C10 g5]. The stems below are paraphrases.

The Foundation article explains how to build a list of prior questions. It supports the last stem in move 6 [15 C14 g5].

The method differs from a normal talk. It draws first principles out in order. It exposes hidden assumptions and separates knowledge from ignorance. It limits gut feeling and strong emotional reactions [1 C11 g5].

## Gap check

The Guide checklist has nine element prompts: goal, information, inferences, concepts, assumptions, implications, point of view, the central question and context [3 C04 g5]. The notes pair no move with goal, inferences, concepts or context [3 4 C22-C27 g4]. When a node stays stuck after six moves, ask about those four (derived, g4).

## Rules of the method

- Make each question rest on the last answer [3 C08 g5]. Meet every answer with a further question [3 C05 g5].
- The spirit counts more than the letter [3 C09 g5]. The moves are not a strict ladder. One answer can open a move you did not plan [4 C21 g3]. The default order is the FS order, 1 to 6.
- The Guide pages the notes read give no rule for when to end a discussion [3 C65 g5]. Use the stop rule in step 4 of `SKILL.md`.

## Move 5 stays narrow

Ask only what an error in this part would cost. The answer sets the depth and the gate. It is a cost, not a chain. It lands in `condition` and in the gate, never in a cell of its own.

The chain "and then what" belongs to `second_order_thinking`. Do not add a next-event line to a node (`skills/phase_2/CRITERIA.md`, check `second_order`). If the next event is the lesson, write `so: yes` in the `evidence` cell and leave the chain to `second_order_thinking`.

## Ask or answer

- The node is the user's own belief or design. Ask one move, wait for the answer, then ask the next. This skill is inline so that it can ask. A forked agent cannot (`.rules/context-isolation.md`, rule 2).
- The node comes from a map or a source. Answer each move from opened sources. Grade each answer. Mark the gaps (house). A forked phase 2 writer cannot ask him, so it always works this way.
